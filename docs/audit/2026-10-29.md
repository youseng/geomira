## 1. What was done

consumer, projective images near the sent line, share links and autosave. The other covered
conic thresholds, the type decision, exports, derived layers and drags. Each finding was
reproduced by a script. The rest of the list was checked directly: flooding and performance
with conics, the single file without workers, mutation, and the flaky tests.

## 2. The hunt list against what was covered

1. **Ideal points and the line at infinity in every consumer.**
   - Claims: finding 1. Its exact form and NDG items are sound. A claim on an ideal point is
     Undefined, never invalid. The cross-ratio residuals are dimensionless.
   - Scene: ideal points are listed "at infinity, direction θ°", and two rays are drawn as two
     clipped pieces.
   - Exports (TikZ, SVG, Asymptote, PNG): ideal points are warnings, never coordinates. Segments
     crossing ℓ become two rays.
   - Discovery: its pools exclude ideal points.
   - Editor: dragging an ideal point is refused, and a point ↔ ideal change counts as a kind
     change.
   - Share links and autosave round-trip a document with ideal points, both map kinds, an image
     layer, conics, a conic glider and the cross-ratio claims.
   - All of these were checked by scripts and found sound.
2. **Projective images near the sent line.** Finding 2. The image-of-range property now holds
   for segments, rays both ways, two rays and ranges from infinity, under maps with det of both
   signs; polygons and angle marks were covered too (c2de397). A line or an end within the band
   of ℓ is Undefined (ambiguous).
3. **Conic degeneracy thresholds and the type near parabolas.** Finding 4. The degeneracy
   measure and the kernel/scene agreement on the band were checked and are sound.
4. **Conic exports.**
   - Sound: a hyperbola gives one path per branch, no chord crosses the gap between branches, a
     conic far outside the view is "outside", and every exported number is finite.
   - Finding 9 for the default view.
5. **Conics in derived layers and drag-through.** Findings 7 and 8. The direct drag of a conic
   glider was already right.
6. **Discovery flooding with conics and projective layers.**
   - No flooding: no spurious fact with conics, nor in Run 16a's projective layer test.
   - Not flooding but starvation (open, §3): on the 150-object figure of
     `e2e/fallback-discovery.spec.ts` with conics added, the 20 s budget ends in the conconic
     scan with 0 facts; without conics, 8.
7. **150-object performance with conics** (Node, 20 s with stop, the e2e figure):
   - longest step: 91.4 ms on the baseline, 28.6 ms with conics, 46.6 ms with conics and a
     projective layer (299 objects);
   - steps over 16 ms: 10 / 2 / 14 of about 170,000.
   - The browser gate (ADR-0037) runs on the baseline figure only; no conic gate was added.
8. **The single-file build and the no-worker fallback.** Sound. The new end-to-end test
   (30148c1) covers conic ∩ circle, foci, a projective image, an SVG of them and a claim tested
   on the page, with Web Workers removed, from file://. It passed locally in Chromium; the
   deploy workflow runs it in all three engines.
9. **The 16b queue.**
   - Findings 6, 10, 13 and 14.
   - Mutation:
     - kernel-b (66.19 % on main): survivors in proj-lines, proj-apply and conic-pairs, killed
       by behaviour tests (c2de397): 77.97 % on the branch nightly (40878931952).
     - discovery catch-all (73.03 %): survivors in harmonic-sections and harmonic-maps, killed
       by rule tests (a9672c2): 78.45 %. Three of the hand-made mutants survive and are equivalent: the
       distinctness check, or the inversion's centre and fixed circle, already decide.
     - Run 16c's new files joined the shards (c32c72b): kernel-f 99.68 %, kernel-h 87.42 %, but
       kernel-j 68.12 % (conic-pencil.ts). 769dbfc kills its Newton and accuracy survivors; much
       of the rest is fallback code no constructed input reaches.
   - The kernel package is at 88.56 %, not back above 93 % (89.98 % on main; the new pencil
     code is the difference). Every package passes its gate. Details in STATUS.md.

## 3. Open after the audit

- **Candidate order is decided on rounding.** Conic ∩ conic orders candidates by x, then y.
  Candidates whose x values are equal up to rounding are ordered by that rounding, not by y. No
  selector depends on the order; only the GUI's written other(P, Q, R) follows it. Recorded,
  not changed.
- **The near-infinity zone of conic ∩ conic** comes from line ∩ conic's asymptotic-direction
  band (the line through the far point), not from `finish`'s distance test. It lies within an
  order of magnitude of scale/BAND (`kernel/test/conic-pencil-behaviour.test.ts`).

- **Discovery starvation with conics** (§2.6): a performance shape of ADR-0035's budgets
  (candidates per kind before tests), not a wrong result. Reproduction: the e2e figure plus
  `D = (6, 6)`, `E = (-2, 4)`, `F = (2, -3)`, two conics through A, B, C, D and E (or F), their
  fourth common point; "What is special?" finds nothing in 20 s. Proposal for the maintainer
  (Requests): test the first candidates of every kind before the long scans, or give conconic a
  smaller per-kind budget.
- **The pole of a line within the band of a conic's centre** is a far finite point. The test is
  EPS without a band, as for circles (ADR-0042 decision 9), so the behaviour is by design. On
  an ill-conditioned projective image (the centre known to 2.6·10⁻⁸) the pole of the axis is a
  point at 10¹⁵. Recorded, not changed.
- The slice test needs Node ≥ 22.19 (`process.threadCpuUsage`); the engines field says
  ≥ 22.12 (ADR-0044).
- Discovery's branch fixes keep two-candidate sites only (ADR-0043): conic ∩ conic is not a
  branch-fix site.
- audit 2026-10-23 §3: unchanged. The audit found none of those bugs in Run 16 code.
